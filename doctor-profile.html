About Dr. Tariq Lone

Dr. Tariq Lone is a senior Orthodontist and Dental Surgeon based in Anantnag with over 12 years of specialized clinical experience. After graduating with honors in Dental Surgery, he completed his Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics.

His private clinic, Kashmir Smile Dental Spa, was established to bring modern painless dental procedures to South Kashmir. He specializes in clear aligners, complex root canal therapy under magnification, pediatric preventative dentistry, and full-mouth rehabilitation.
